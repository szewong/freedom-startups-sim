A simulation, not data. Its value is the counterfactual: the same founder down every path, which no observational study can produce because the founders who raise are not the founders who don't.

The browser engine is a second implementation of the Python model, kept honest by scripts/validate_js_engine.cjs, which fails if the two disagree. Method, calibration, sweeps and retractions are in FINDINGS.md.
